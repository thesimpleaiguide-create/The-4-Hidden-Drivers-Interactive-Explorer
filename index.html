Driver 01

Fear & Anxiety

SME Core: Role Dismissal as Fear of Abandonment
SME Principle:

"My GP always sees me. Why am I being fobbed off?" Fear expresses itself as hostility, demands, or dismissal of your role — not personal attack.

10% Above The Waterline (Observable Presentation)

Role dismissal, demanding to speak only to their GP, questioning qualifications, cold hostility towards coordinators or link workers.

Spoken Patient Example:

"Hang on a minute. A care coordinator? What is that? I've seen Dr. Evans for fifteen years. Now I'm passed off to someone who isn't even a doctor? I want my GP."

90% Beneath The Waterline (The Hidden Driver)

Acute fear and attachment anxiety. The patient relies on their regular GP as their safety anchor. Being redirected feels like being dropped from the system.

The Patient's Unspoken Thought:

"Dr. Evans is the only one who knows my complex history. If I get fobbed off to an assistant, I will slip through the cracks."

Autonomic Trigger & Threat State

Separation panic fires the amygdala. Perceived loss of primary care support is experienced as an existential threat to safety.

The Clinician's 10-Second Verbal Pivot:

"David, you've known Dr. Evans a long time and I don't want to get in the way of that. I'm not replacing him — my whole job is making sure what he plans for you actually happens without delays."

Key: Validate the GP relationship immediately. Never debate your qualifications.
